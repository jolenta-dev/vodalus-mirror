What is this?

This is vodalus.org.

What is that?

Vodalus is my stab at trying to revive an internet I don't remember
I started Vodalus just to have a place to screw around with stuff I wanted to try and to have a place to hang out that wasn't hellish or riddled with enshittification AI-slop.
Vodalus is also a tribute to its namesake. Vodalus is the rebellious optimate fighting the Autarch in Gene Wolfe's Book of the New Sun. Book of the New Sun is a big deal to me (obviously) and its very seldom mentioned online, so I wanted to give a home to some of the ideas and share them with the world to get more people looking at one of my favorite pieces of art.

What is Vodalus running off of?

After an insane run of luck, I happened into a Xeon VM host at work. Vodalus (and the vodalus media server) now have their own dedicated VMs running a Proxmox cluster. Specs:


Proxmox host:

  • CPU: Intel(R) Xeon(R) D-2143IT CPU @ 2.20GHz
  • RAM: 64GB DDR4
  • GPU: Nvidia Tesla P9 (coming soon TM)

Vodalus VM:

  • CPU: 2 CPU Cores
  • RAM: 8GB DDR4
  • OS: Arch Linux (LTS)

I have a Cisco ISR-4431 (did I mention I love enterprise networking?) working as my main router, uplinked to two Cisco Catalyst 2960X switches that do distribution. There's a double NAT happening and a pretty involved list of ACLs (good luck) since I run this on my personal IP. A Pi 4B 8GB runs DNS and a Tor bridge and some other light services, but most of it runs off diuturna.

The chat is an entire API (internally named Katharine). It's currently really a custom-built solution for Vodalus, but I'd like to open source it in the future when it's more feature-complete and extensible (and not awful to work with)

What is Vodalus's stance on AI-generated code?

It's complicated.
This is the typical vodalus "AI" workflow (if you're curious):

  1. I have an idea for a feature/page/&c.
  2. I have an LLM use a skill to ask me a bunch of questions to generate a PRD
  3. That PRD is then taken by another LLM to create issues on GitLab
  4. I implement the issues and goals. I write all the code myself with Supermaven autocompletes (sometimes, sometimes it annoys me too much). If I get really stuck, I'll ask for help.

This site isn't vibe-coded, but I'm not going to act like AI can't be a crazy timesaver in a solo dev enviroment. I have a Cursor subscription, I will use it, but I will never go to a model and say "make me a cool page!". There's a lot of design and thought I put into the ideas that get put through.
