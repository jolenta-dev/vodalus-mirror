What is this?

This is vodalus.org.

What is that?

Vodalus is my stab at trying to revive an internet I don't remember
I started Vodalus just to have a place to screw around with stuff I wanted to try and to have a place to hang out that wasn't hellish or riddle with enshittification AI-slop.
Vodalus is also a tribute to its namesake. Vodalus is the rebellious optimate fighting the Autarch in Gene Wolfe's Book of the New Sun. Book of the New Sun is a big deal to me (obviously) and its very seldom mentioned online, so I wanted to give a home to some of the ideas and share them with the world to get more people looking at one of my favorite pieces of art.

How can I support Vodalus?

Right now, the biggest thing you can do is chat, gamble, and tell your friends. We don't have a lot of users period, let alone regulars. I'd like to have many more, but that's only possible if people share the site.

What's the update theme for <\Month>?

I reveal themes when the features are ready. VIPs usually get to know ahead of time what I'm planning, but sometimes even I don't know what's gonna hit prod and when.

This chat seems really cool, can I use it for my site?

The chat is an entire API (internally named Katharine). It's currently really a custom-built solution for Vodalus, but I'd like to open souce it in the future when it's more feature-complete and extensible (and not awful to work with)

How do I contact you?

DMs are really the main way as of now. I tried to set up SMTP for this domain and Comcast blocked me from sending/receiving emails and I refuse to pay for email hosting, so no email right now.

I've heard of a bug bounty, how does that work?

Since Vodalus's backend isn't totally public (since I don't trust people), this isn't a traditional find-the-code-and-fix-it kind of bounty It comes down mostly to a judgement call on my end so let me give you examples of what people have found that I've rewarded for:

  • Major typos
  • Feature-breaking bugs (i.e. users can't redeem rewards or entering the wrong password still lets you login)
  • Worthwhile user code contributions
For these I usually give out phrases (since they're the main economy currently), but if you want something else (within the realm of the site, no money they pay me like shit), we can probably work something out.

What is Vodalus's stance on AI-generated code

It's complicated.
I am, by no stretch, good at Javascript, HTML, or CSS, and the AIs are. I mainly use Cursor as an IDE and I try to solve all issues myself first before handing them off to AI. Occasionally a feature is built top to bottom with AI and then manually reviewed for internal consistency and quality. AI is often used for wrote tasks (renaming API endpoints, changing variable names, refactoring, &c.), and I'd like this to be the main way it touches the codebase. All of this is to say, AI is used, but this is not a vibe-coded website. I'm one person working on this in my free time, if using an LLM saves me an hour on a feature implementation, that's a worthwhile value proposition to me.
If that bothers you, sorry, but it's <\current year> and that's the reality.
